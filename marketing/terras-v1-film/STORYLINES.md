# Storylines

Picked: **A**.

## A. One order, one thread (picked)
Device: one object across the plant. Form: object-to-object chain.
One sales order line (10,000 yd, NAVY, L-72 (70-74)) becomes a Production Run, a Kartu Kerja with a QR, a scan onto a loom card, a dye bath, a PU- carton label, a verified Surat Jalan that dispatches. The viewer leaves knowing the system covers the whole floor, not the paperwork.
First 2 s: the real SO row, the yardage punched in large.
Beats: hook (the order) → tension (six departments, one order) → reveal "Terras ERP" → proof: plan, scan, weave, dye, pack, verify → payoff: dispatch, genealogy unrolling behind it → claim "Every lot, order to truck" → end card.
Look: the product's own Classic (XP) chrome on #ece9d8, XP blue #0058e6 as the second world, XP green #2d7a2d rationed to payoff and end card.
Sound: no music source available (no HeyGen, no local MusicGen); bundled SFX on visible actions only.

## B. The paper floor
Device: life before and after. Form: match cuts.
The factory's own paper forms each become the same document printed from Terras with a QR that gets scanned. Needs a photo of a real paper form.

## C. Trace it back
Device: the result first. Form: one continuous zoom.
A carton label is scanned; the camera dives backwards through lot genealogy (carton ← dyed lot ← greige ← warp beam ← yarn lot) and ends on "Every lot, traced."
