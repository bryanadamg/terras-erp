# Storylines

Brief: focus on the features shipped since v1.0, while saying what Terras ERP is for (the whole mill on one record, order to truck).

Picked: **B**.

## A. Who touched this lot?
Device: the result first. Form: object-to-object chain, walking backwards.
A buyer complains about one carton; the manager scans its label and the film walks back: Surat Jalan printed by / verified by → greige lot drew from several lots → the loom's tuner → the beam's drawing-in operator → the yarn receipt with the supplier's Surat Jalan. Claim "Every lot. Every hand. On record."

## B. One day at the mill (picked)
Device: a moment in someone's day, hour by hour. Form: a flight through one world (the live floor-plan dashboard as the home stage).
06:00 yarn received into two stores at VAT-inclusive prices → 08:00 beam mounted, drawing-in operator logged → 10:00 loom into Tuning, tuner picked → 13:00 output logged from several lots, its label opens → 15:00 Kartu Picking sorted by location → 17:00 the loading deck flags an unprinted Surat Jalan → 18:00 the day goes to Excel. Claim "The whole mill. One system."

## C. Paper and system, one thing
Device: life before and after. Form: match cuts on the barcode.
The mill's own papers (SPK Produksi, work card, Kartu Picking, Surat Jalan) each scanned, each landing on its exact row; the system stamps what was printed. Claim "Every paper finds its row."
